Relaxing winter bath: salts, oils and accessories for a cozy ritual

When it's cold and dark early, a hot bath is one of the most comforting moments of the week. A few well-chosen products turn it into a relaxing ritual, as long as you think about your skin.

Bath products

  • Bath salts: popular for that relaxing feel, scented or unscented.
  • Bath oil: leaves a soft film on the skin, handy for dry skin.
  • Bath bombs: fizzy and fragrant, great as a small gift.
  • Bath milk: makes the water soft and milky.

Accessories that make a difference

  • A bath pillow for your neck.
  • A bathtub tray for a book or a hot drink.
  • A thick, fluffy towel or robe for afterwards.

Good habits

  • Warm rather than scalding water, and 15 to 20 minutes is plenty.
  • Bath oil makes the tub slippery: rinse it afterwards.
  • Apply body lotion or cream right after.
  • Pick fragrance-free products if your skin is sensitive.

Does a bath dry out skin?

Very hot water and long soaks can. Keep the water warm and moisturize right after.
