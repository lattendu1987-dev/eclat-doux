Beauty gift sets: Christmas ideas people actually love

A beauty gift set is an easy present, as long as you choose well. Here are ideas by type and what to check to please for sure.

Choosing well

  • Stick to safe bets: hand care, bath and shower, candles, accessories.
  • For fragrance, give a scent the person already wears, or a discovery set of minis.
  • Check ingredients if the person has sensitive skin or known allergies.
  • Look at the estimated delivery date before ordering for Christmas.

Ideas by profile

  • For someone who loves pampering: a skincare set or a home spa kit.
  • For someone who has everything: accessories (smoothing brush, jade roller, skincare headband).
  • On a small budget: a hand cream and lip balm trio, always useful in winter.
