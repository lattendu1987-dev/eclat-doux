Dry feet in winter: the care to choose for soft heels

In winter, feet shut in boots and dry indoor air often leave heels rough. A simple routine, a few minutes a week, is often enough to get softer feet back.

Useful products

  • A rich, fast-absorbing foot cream, applied in the evening.
  • A gentle foot file or pumice stone, once a week, on dry skin or right after a shower.
  • Cotton or moisturizing socks for the night.
  • A lukewarm foot soak for a relaxing moment.

What to look for

  • Popular ingredients: urea, glycerin, shea butter.
  • A texture that absorbs well, so you don't slip when walking.
  • A gentle file rather than a cutting tool.

Good habits

  • File gently, without pressing or going until it feels tender.
  • Dry well between the toes after showering.
  • If skin cracks, bleeds or hurts, ask a pharmacist or podiatrist.
