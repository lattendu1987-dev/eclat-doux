Body scrub in winter: how to choose one for soft skin without overdoing it

In winter, body skin can look dull and feel rough under warm clothes. A gentle scrub followed by good moisturizing helps skin feel smoother, as long as you don't overdo it.

Main types

  • Sugar scrub: grains that dissolve, fairly gentle, suits most skin.
  • Salt scrub: more intense; avoid on irritated skin or right after shaving.
  • Exfoliating mitt or sponge: affordable and reusable; wash and dry it well.
  • Fine-grain gentle formula: for sensitive skin.

What to look for

  • Grain size: the finer, the gentler.
  • A nourishing base (oils, butters) if your skin is dry.
  • Light or no fragrance if your skin is sensitive.

Good habits

  • Once a week is usually enough, on wet skin, in small circles.
  • Avoid the face, irritated areas and freshly shaved skin.
  • Follow with a body lotion or cream.

FAQ

How often should I scrub?

Once a week suits most skin. If your skin turns red or feels tight, scrub less often.
