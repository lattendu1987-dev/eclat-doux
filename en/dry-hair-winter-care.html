Dry hair in winter: the care to choose when hats and heating take their toll

Hats, cold wind and dry indoor air: in winter, lots of hair turns dry, staticky and brittle. A few well-chosen products and small habits are often enough to get softness back.

Useful products

  • Conditioner after every wash: it detangles and smooths the lengths.
  • A nourishing mask once a week, on the lengths and ends.
  • A little oil or serum on the ends, on dry or damp hair.
  • Leave-in care for thick or curly hair.

What to look for

  • The hair type on the label: fine, thick, curly, color-treated.
  • Light textures for fine hair, richer ones for thick hair.
  • A gentle shampoo, used no more often than needed.

Good habits

  • Lower the hairdryer heat and use a heat protectant.
  • A satin-lined hat or pillowcase reduces friction and static.
  • Rinse with lukewarm rather than very hot water.
