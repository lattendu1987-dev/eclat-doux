Hand cream for dry hands: choosing the right one for your day

Frequent washing, hand sanitizer and cold weather: hands are often the first to suffer in winter. The right cream mostly depends on when and how you use it.

Pick the texture

  • Daytime: a light texture that absorbs quickly and leaves no marks on your phone or keyboard.
  • Night: a rich cream or balm, possibly with cotton gloves while you sleep.
  • Very dry hands: look for glycerin, urea, shea butter or ceramides in the ingredient list.

Nice extras

  • A purse-size tube to reapply after every wash.
  • Fragrance-free formulas for sensitive skin.
  • A pump bottle for the bathroom: you use it more when it's within reach.

Good habits

Dry your hands well after washing, wear gloves outside and apply cream on clean hands. If skin cracks, turns red or itches, ask a pharmacist or doctor.
