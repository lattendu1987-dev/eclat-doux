Winter face moisturizer: choosing by skin type

In winter, tight or flaky skin is common. Switching moisturizers for the cold season may be enough, as long as you choose by skin type.

By skin type

  • Dry skin: a rich cream with plant butters, ceramides or glycerin.
  • Combination skin: a gel-cream that hydrates without making the T-zone shiny.
  • Sensitive skin: a short, fragrance-free formula, ideally dermatologist-tested.

Popular ingredients

  • Hyaluronic acid and glycerin: they help skin hold on to water.
  • Ceramides: they support the skin's barrier role.
  • Niacinamide: valued for the comfort of sensitive skin.

Good habits

  • Apply on slightly damp skin after cleansing.
  • Patch-test a new product on a small area for a few days.
  • Keep using sunscreen on sunny days, even in winter.

FAQ

Do I need a different cream in winter?

Often, yes: many people switch to a richer texture when it's cold. If your skin feels tight, your summer cream is probably too light.
