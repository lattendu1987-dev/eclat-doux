Beauty advent calendar: how to choose the right one

Beauty advent calendars have become a December must. But contents vary a lot: here's what to check before ordering, and when to order.

Order early

The most popular calendars often sell out in November. To open it on December 1st, order by mid-November.

What to look at

  • The detailed contents: most brands publish the product list.
  • Sizes: minis or full sizes. Full sizes are rarer but last longer.
  • Type: skincare, makeup, fragrance, or a mix.
  • Your skin type: check that the skincare products suit you.

As a gift

  • Pick a brand the person already likes.
  • A skincare calendar is easier to get right than a makeup one, whose shades are more personal.
