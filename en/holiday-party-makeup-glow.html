Holiday party makeup: the products for a glowy look that lasts all night

For holiday dinners and New Year's Eve, you want makeup that glows and lasts from dinner to midnight. A few well-chosen products are enough, no need to replace everything.

Key products

  • A hydrating or smoothing primer, especially if skin feels tight in winter.
  • A highlighter (cream or powder) on the tops of the cheekbones for glow.
  • A long-wear lipstick, or a matching lip liner so it doesn't bleed.
  • A setting spray to help makeup last longer.

What to look for

  • Shades that suit your skin tone: ask for samples when you can.
  • Cream textures for dry skin, powders for combination skin.
  • A compact, versatile palette rather than lots of single products.

Good habits

  • Moisturize well before applying makeup.
  • Keep your lipstick and a small mirror in your bag for touch-ups.
  • Remove makeup gently at the end of the night, even when it's late.
