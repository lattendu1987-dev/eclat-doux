Bedroom humidifier: a skin ally when the heating is on

Indoor heating dries the air, and your skin, lips and hair feel it. A humidifier can make the air more comfortable, if you choose it well and keep it clean.

Two main types

  • Ultrasonic: quiet, ideal for the bedroom. Use suitable water to limit white dust.
  • Evaporative: naturally self-regulating, but often slightly louder.

What to check

  • The room size stated by the maker.
  • Tank capacity: it sets how long it runs overnight.
  • Auto shut-off when the tank is empty.
  • A built-in or separate hygrometer: humidity around 40 to 60 % is a common target.
  • Easy cleaning: a wide-opening tank is easier to clean.

Cleaning

Empty and rinse the tank regularly and follow the maker's cleaning instructions: a poorly maintained unit can encourage mold. Also avoid keeping the room too humid.
