Gentle makeup remover: how to choose one when skin feels tight in winter

In winter, a harsh makeup remover can leave skin feeling tight. Choosing a gentle texture and the right method helps keep skin comfortable, morning and night.

Main textures

  • Cleansing oil or balm: melts makeup, pleasant on dry skin. Rinses off with lukewarm water.
  • Cleansing milk: soft and comfortable, removed with a pad or water.
  • Micellar water: quick and handy; rinse it off if your skin is sensitive.
  • Gentle gel cleanser: as a second step for combination skin.

What to look for

  • Your skin type: dry, combination or sensitive.
  • A fragrance-free formula if your skin reacts easily.
  • An eye-safe product if you wear mascara.

Good habits

  • Massage gently, without rubbing, then rinse with lukewarm water.
  • Use soft, reusable cleansing pads.
  • Apply moisturizer right after, on slightly damp skin.
