Satin accessories: protecting your hair at night and under hats in winter

In winter, hats, scarves and dry air often leave hair staticky and tangled. Satin or silk accessories glide more than cotton and reduce friction, at night and during the day.

Useful accessories

  • Satin or silk pillowcase: less friction overnight.
  • Satin sleep bonnet: handy for long, curly or coily hair.
  • Satin scrunchies: hold hair without dents or breakage.
  • Satin-lined winter beanie: stay warm without static.

Satin or silk?

  • Satin is usually polyester: affordable and easy to wash.
  • Silk (mulberry) is a natural fiber, softer but pricier and more delicate.
  • Check the label: "satin" is a weave, not a material.

Good habits

  • Wash pillowcases on a low temperature, inside out, in a mesh bag.
  • Skip fabric softener, which can damage silk.
  • Tie long hair in a loose braid at night.
