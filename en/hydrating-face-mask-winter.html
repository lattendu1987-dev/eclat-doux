Hydrating face mask: how to choose one for a cozy winter ritual

In winter, a hydrating mask is a little ritual many people enjoy for more comfortable skin and a relaxing moment. There are several textures: here's how to choose based on your skin and your schedule.

Main types

  • Sheet mask: quick and handy, ideal for a relaxing break.
  • Cream mask: rich and comfortable, suits dry skin.
  • Gel mask: fresh and light, popular with combination skin.
  • Overnight mask: applied as the last evening step and rinsed in the morning.

What to look for

  • Your skin type: dry, combination or sensitive.
  • Popular ingredients: hyaluronic acid, glycerin, ceramides.
  • A fragrance-free formula if your skin is sensitive.

Good habits

  • Apply on clean skin, once or twice a week.
  • Follow the stated time.
  • Patch-test a new mask on a small area first.
