Lip balm for chapped lips: how to pick one that really protects

Cold, wind and dry indoor air quickly take a toll on lips. A good balm forms a protective layer, as long as you choose it well and apply it at the right time.

What makes a good balm

  • Ingredients that form a protective film: beeswax or plant wax, shea butter, lanolin, petrolatum.
  • Fragrance- and flavor-free if your lips are sensitive: some fragrances can irritate.
  • SPF for skiing or sunny days: winter sun reflects off snow.
  • A handy format: a stick for your pocket, a pot for a richer evening treatment.

Good habits

  • Apply before going out in the cold, not only once lips are damaged.
  • Avoid licking your lips: evaporating saliva dries them even more.
  • At night, a thick layer as an overnight mask helps lips feel comfortable again.
  • If cracks persist or bleed, ask a pharmacist for advice.
